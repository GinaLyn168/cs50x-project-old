Shadow Girl was one of the colored spirits a fairy, who calls herself Fae, was keeping watch over. Fae was protecting those spirits from her evil twin brother, who calls himself "The Dark Lord" and was abducting people from the mortal world--the "Normal World"--so he can suck out their souls and absorb them to become more powerful (fortunately, he is still figuring out how to do that last part). Dark Lord also tainted many pure spirits with his magic and turned them into his servants (whenever a being in the Magic World dies, their spirit is released into the world as a shining orb of light and is free to drift around, and naturally as time passes the spirit gets cleansed and purified back into a blank slate and can be reborn into another being; however, for more powerful beings such as fairies, when they die their spirits are colored and take a long time to get purified back to white). Since colored spirits are more powerful, Fae is trying her best to keep them from being tainted, as that would give TDL a lot more power.

On a fateful day in autumn, Fae visited the Normal World to obtain some supplies to recharge her own magic. She gathered some oranges and was about to return when a car sped by and splashed water from a nearby puddle all over her. Startled, she fell and dropped the basket she was carrying, and the oranges inside it rolled all over the street. A kind young girl saw this and and rushed to help her. Seeing this, Fae decided to reward her kindness by gifting her a unique companion of her own--secretly, she was thinking that it would also be safer for one of the spirits she is guarding to leave with a kind person to the Normal World, as that could potentially prevent them from being found by TDL, and a kind soul should be more enough to protect them from spiritual harm.
She led the girl, whose name was Rita, to her abode in the Magic World, and bid her choose a spirit from her collection.
Rita chose the orange one. Fae released the spirit and let it possess her shadow.
And that was how Shadow Girl was born.
The two were practically inseperable. Rita took her home and somehow talked her parents into letting her keep a supernatural being as a friend. Being literally her shadow, Shadow Girl followed her everywhere, and they talked about anything and everything. Rita was happy to have a close friend she can share her secrets with, and Shadow Girl--who got her simplistic name from Rita--was happy to befriend a human and remained loyal to Rita.

One day in late spring, the two friends hung out alone on a bench at the park. Shadow Girl was looking away from Rita and enjoying the warm sunshine, when she suddenly felt a strong nauseous sensation, as if her very soul was being sucked out of her body. She passed out, and when she woke up again, Rita had disappeared. (Truth is, one of TDL's spirit servants that had been assigned to the Normal World to collect souls had abducted her while SG was distracted.)
They couldn't find her. Rita was gone without a trace. Her parents were in dispair, and Shadow Girl was devestated. She felt guilty that she hadn't protected Rita.
When months had passed and Rita still wasn't found, Shadow Girl decided to set out into the world in search for her. She traveled everywhere for a long time, meeting many people and seeing many sights, before getting herself into some trouble: one of TDL's spirit servants who had been given a little more magic to had orders from him to either find someone else in the Normal World to abduct or to find and capture Shadow Girl specifically, and when they met each other, Shadow Girl unsuspectingly interacted with it and got her mind possessed by it. Thankfully, Fae was able to access SG's mind due to the connection she had previously build with SG's spirit while she was still in a jar, and she killed/passified the spirit and gave SG a clue as to where to find Rita and all the other abducted people. Rita, freed from her mental prision, set out at once to her destination.

She met a couple unique people along the way who could help her get Rita back. They became her friends as the journey progressed, and they fought alongside each other when they finally met TDL and his chamber of abducted souls just as he was going to perform his ritual to absorb them on a full-moon night. When they finally succeeded in slaying TDL's body, everyone was heavily injured, but Fae was able to access his soul and confront him for the last time, and she absorbed his soul (but kept it within her independently so he still technically exists) and all his magic and healed/revived everyone as well as released the trapped people. Everyone reunited and it was a happy ending.
After the incident (skipping some details here), the hero friends all split and went their different ways, but they still missed the friendship they shared and the adventures they used to go on together. Their Creator, seeing this, stepped in and gathered them to a special place where they could live together and go on missions of hunting down anomalies together and still visit their other families and friends, as well as visit a vast expanse of different dimensions.
